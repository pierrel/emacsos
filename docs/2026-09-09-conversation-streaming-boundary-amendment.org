#+TITLE: Conversation streaming size-bound amendment
#+DATE: <2026-09-09>

* Correction

The September 8 design incorrectly assigned a 16 KiB decoded-delta bound to
Assist. Assist emits at most 7 KiB UTF-8 model-text chunks. =assist-web.el=
independently accepts at most 16 KiB of decoded UTF-8 text for one delta, and
continues to cap one whole SSE event and one raw process-filter callback at
64 KiB. Assist separately caps encoded SSE records at 48 KiB.

* Lineage

This amends [[file:2026-09-08-conversation-interface-streaming.org][one native
conversation interface]]. The earlier record remains historical. This
correction assigns each bound to the server emission, client delta validation,
or client transport layer that actually owns it.
