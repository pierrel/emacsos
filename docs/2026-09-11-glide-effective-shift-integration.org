* Integration

The pinned wvkbd revision retains the accepted glide word-editing behavior and
uses the same effective modifier calculation for key emission and separator
replacement. Shifted numeric punctuation therefore replaces the automatic
separator whether Shift is latched or forced by the key. The calculation also
preserves the existing forced-modifier and reset-modifier behavior.
