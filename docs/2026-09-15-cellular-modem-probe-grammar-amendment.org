#+title: Cellular modem probe grammar amendment
#+date: 2026-09-15

* Historical supersessions

This amendment replaces only the successful-probe classification in
[[file:2026-09-15-cellular-modem-recovery.org][Cellular-on recovery for a missing ModemManager object]], section /Recovery
contract/.  That record remains the historical design for the recovery flow.

* Correction

A successful =mmcli -L= exit authorizes ordinary cellular activation only when
every output line is a modem-object listing and at least one listing exists.
The exact single-line =No modems were found= result authorizes the bounded
fixed-service recovery.  Empty, mixed, or otherwise unrecognized output fails
conservatively without activation or restart.

* Verification

The focused root-helper regression supplies one valid modem listing followed by
an unrecognized line and requires =not-connected:failed= without a service
restart.  Existing cases retain healthy activation, exact no-modem recovery,
and conservative handling of failed, timed-out, and unrecognized probes.
