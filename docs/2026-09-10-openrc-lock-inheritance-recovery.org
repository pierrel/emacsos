#+title: OpenRC transaction-lock inheritance recovery
#+date: 2026-09-10

* Lineage

This follows [[file:2026-09-10-openrc-session-gate-recovery.org][the session-gate recovery amendment]] and
[[file:2026-09-10-openrc-v1-transaction-recovery.org][the version-1 transaction recovery]].  It records a second live deployment
failure found while installing their corrected payload.

* Observed boundary

The UI was healthy after rollback, but the next update failed with “keyboard
install lock is busy.”  The OpenRC supervisor and its logger children held the
lock.  They had inherited a coordination-lock descriptor from the updater that
started them, extending the lock from one bounded transaction to the lifetime
of the UI.

Two launch paths had the same defect: the OpenRC updater retained its keyboard
lock on descriptor 7, and the standalone keyboard activator retained its
install lock on descriptor 9.

* Contract

Before invoking OpenRC to start, stop, or restart the persistent UI, both
transaction helpers close descriptors 7, 8, and 9 in the child command.  These
are the fixed descriptors for the keyboard, boot-mode, and session-install
locks.  Redirection applies only to the child, so the transaction caller keeps
its own locks until it finishes while the supervisor and its descendants
cannot inherit them.

The keyboard fixture rejects any fake OpenRC launch that can still see one of
those three locks.  The full keyboard activation and interruption matrix then
passes.  The OpenRC installer fixture applies the same assertion to session
updates and passes its install, rollback, reconciliation, and update cases.

* Historical supersessions

No earlier statement is superseded.  This adds an omitted child-descriptor
lifetime constraint to the [[file:2026-09-08-glide-modifier-keyboard.org::*Cross-repository interface][glide-modifier cross-repository interface]] and the
[[file:2026-09-10-openrc-session-gate-recovery.org::*Contract][session-gate contract]]: holding a transaction lock in the caller is required,
while letting a persistent OpenRC child inherit that descriptor is forbidden.
