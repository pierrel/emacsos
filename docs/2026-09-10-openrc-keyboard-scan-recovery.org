#+title: OpenRC keyboard process-scan recovery
#+date: 2026-09-10

* Lineage

This follows [[file:2026-09-10-openrc-session-gate-recovery.org][the session-gate recovery amendment]].  It records an ordering defect
found by that amendment's =verify-current= check on the live phone.

* Observed boundary

The gate found the one process with the correct keyboard command, but rejected
the scan before reaching the executable-digest check.  Its process-scan
function implicitly returned the result of the final loop-body predicate.  The
live OpenRC cgroup ended with an unrelated =sleep 30= heartbeat process, so the
function's success depended on cgroup process order.

* Contract

Within its existing 256-entry bound, the scan emits every exact keyboard match
and explicitly succeeds after reaching end of file.  Its caller remains
responsible for requiring exactly one emitted PID and verifying that PID's
executable digest.  Unrelated processes before or after the keyboard do not
affect a scan within that bound.

The keyboard fixture places an unrelated =emacsos-lab= process after the sole
keyboard in the synthetic cgroup and requires =verify-current= to pass.  The
existing zero-keyboard and two-keyboard cases continue to fail.

* Historical supersessions

No earlier statement is superseded.  This makes the ordering-independent
“exactly one keyboard” contract in the
[[file:2026-09-10-openrc-session-gate-recovery.org::*Contract][session-gate amendment]] explicit in the implementation.
