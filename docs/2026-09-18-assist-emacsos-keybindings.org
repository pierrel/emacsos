#+title: Assist and EmacsOS keybindings

* Outcome

=C-c a= owns Assist integration: =l= opens the native Assist thread list and
=t= invokes the existing catalog completion, with its list fallback.  =C-c e=
owns EmacsOS: =c= opens ordinary chat, =m= opens SMS conversations, and =s=
stages an SMS.  PinePhone-only leaves remain under =C-c e=.

Every chat surface sends with =C-<return>= and retains its physical =RET=
behavior.  Mode-local actions are direct: =C-c C-r= refreshes, =C-c C-k=
aborts or detaches, =C-c C-o= opens a backend-owned HTTP(S) object, =C-c C-l=
loads older canonical history, and =C-c C-f= forgets a file-backed chat.

The conversation action table remains the ownership boundary.  Object opening
is installed only for canonical Assist, ordinary chat, and file-backed chats;
SMS reports it unavailable even when a synthetic object property is present.
