#+TITLE: Keep the phone awake on external USB power
#+DATE: 2026-10-13

* Corrected policy

Pierre corrected the sleep policy: external power must exempt automatic deep
suspend. Normal display blanking remains allowed. On battery, the existing
worker requests deep suspend 120 seconds after confirmed screen-off. This
successor retains the private wake-input queues, fixed root descriptor handoff,
independent explicit callback guards and pre-initial-wake readiness barrier.

The root admission helper checks only the observed =axp20x-usb= supply. Either
=online=1 or =present=1 skips deep suspend with status 77; both validated values
must be zero before admission. Battery =online= and battery charging status are
not the external-power signal. Root's actual read-only evidence showed USB
=online=1 while the battery also reported =online=1 and =Discharging=. A full
battery and a plugged battery that is discharging must both remain exempt:
=/tmp/root-phone-external-power-evidence-20261013.json=.

The service-user action treats 77 as a normal skip, leaving the panel dark.
The timer keeps its existing blank generation and deadline. A plug before the
deadline vetoes its attempt. Unplug before that deadline uses the remaining
original time. Once a powered attempt has been skipped, the already armed
120-second retry remains: unplug while dark can wait up to 120 seconds for that
retry, without another blank event or a deadline reset. Calls/SMS and deliberate
power-button wake retain their existing guarded actions while plugged.

* Admission construction

The helper checks USB presence before preflight and again after counter
acceptance, before =mem=. It enables and verifies the existing USB supply wake
source before an offline admission. The exact matching kernel's USB plug IRQ
calls =power_supply_changed=, whose kernel source stays active through its work
and advances the wake counter. A plug after the final userspace presence read
therefore belongs to the standard counter/IRQ admission fence.

The additional =present= check is necessary. The driver reads =online= from
VBUS_USED and polls for changes; a plug IRQ may already have completed while
VBUS_PRESENT is one and VBUS_USED is still zero. ONLINE-only checks would leave
a later USED transition unfenced. Requiring absent USB presence ties eligibility
to the physical plug/removal event instead. Exact published source:
[[https://codeberg.org/megi/linux/src/tag/orange-pi-6.13-20250323-0155/drivers/power/supply/axp20x_usb_power.c][AXP USB supply]],
[[https://codeberg.org/megi/linux/src/tag/orange-pi-6.13-20250323-0155/drivers/power/supply/power_supply_core.c][power supply wake source]].

Root's later wake-flag probe timed out and produced no result. This record does
not claim a fresh observed enabled flag from that attempt. Actual deployment
must verify the fixed presence properties and wake source; the helper also
validates the flag after enabling it. Missing or malformed presence state fails
closed. No battery-status heuristic, generic supply scan, new worker, timer or
privilege grant was added.

* Regression coverage

The change adds a fixed USB supply fixture to the existing isolated power
harness.
Its fake battery remains ONLINE=1. Power-policy timer cases invoke the actual
root helper and same-object descriptor handoff; the ordinary status stub is
disabled for those cases. USB-connected Full and Discharging cases require
status 77, no accepted =mem= and an unchanged dark generation. PRESENT=1 with
ONLINE=0 must also skip. Plug during a pending deadline must veto sleep. After
unplug while already dark, the existing retry must eventually admit without
changing the generation; unplug before expiry must retain the original deadline.

A deterministic plug after counter sampling must be rejected by the second
presence check. A plug after that check changes the modeled kernel counter and
must reject the accepted count even after its IRQ source has finished. The model
covers software ordering and the standard counter contract; it does not replace
physical charger/suspend validation. Existing callback/input-queue, lifetime,
startup readiness and error cases continue to run.
