* D5 collision and reconciliation correction

An ordinary two-entry FIFO is not a collision.  Collision provenance belongs
only to adoption that widened a resident queue above the normal two-entry
bound.  A collision therefore remains set for 4→3→2 only when it was already
set, and clears at one.  Restored records accept a noncollision queue of up to
two entries, or a collision queue of two through four entries.

| Behavior | Evidence |
|----------+----------|
| Ordinary two-entry recovery | =test-assist-web-restore-keeps-an-ordinary-two-entry-queue-open= |
| One-plus-one adoption | =test-assist-web-one-plus-one-adoption-keeps-the-ordinary-queue-open= |
| Adopted collision contraction | =test-assist-web-dismiss-contracts-a-public-adoption-collision= |
| Failed reconcile transition | =test-assist-web-reconcile-save-failure-restores-terminals-before-retry= |

The failed reconcile-transition case restores each tentative =reconciling=
entry to =terminal-unreconciled=, retains FIFO order and a fixed retry status,
and sends no GET until the next successful explicit reconciliation attempt.

All other rows of the full D5 matrix remain as recorded in round six.
