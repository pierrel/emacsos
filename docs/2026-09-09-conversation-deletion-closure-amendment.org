#+TITLE: Conversation deleted-thread recovery amendment
#+DATE: <2026-09-09>

* Correction

The deleted-thread closure now clears every provisional insertion marker as
well as the accepted retry tuple.  The visible partial assistant text remains
evidence in the open buffer, but the next Send creates a fresh pending region
and a new canonical thread.  Before the old canonical identity is discarded,
the client adopts =new-thread= as the local draft identity and attempts to
persist the next editable tail.  A successful local cache write preserves that
draft across restart; a write failure leaves the visible buffer usable and is
reported by the cache layer.

Assist also registers status-only observations while they are open.  Thus a
thread deleted during journal saturation or after a process restart still
arrives as the same =closed-set/thread-gone= outcome rather than an ambiguous
disconnected observer.
