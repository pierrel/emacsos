#+title: Glide word editing integration amendment
#+date: 2026-09-11

* Historical supersessions

This supersedes that record's =Integration= pin =4714930=, which lacks the
post-recognition editing unit. The keyboard command and deployment path remain
unchanged.

* Integration

The pinned wvkbd revision emits a recognized glide word followed by Space. Its
immediate plain Backspace retracts that exact word-space unit. Punctuation and
an explicit Space follow the emitted separator, and external focus, cursor, or
hardware edits cannot be observed. No Emacs integration or persistence is added.
Keyboard lifecycle and layout-context resets also disarm the one-shot undo.
