* Integration

The pinned wvkbd revision emits up to 24 glide letters and one balanced Space.
Its immediate plain Backspace is a one-shot retraction of that exact word-space
unit. Punctuation and an explicit Space follow the emitted separator, and
external focus, cursor, or hardware edits cannot be observed. No Emacs
integration or persistence is added. Keyboard lifecycle and layout-context
resets also disarm the one-shot undo.
