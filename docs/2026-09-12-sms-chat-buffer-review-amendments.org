#+title: Native SMS conversation review amendments
#+date: 2026-09-12

* Amendments

The external-keyboard phone keeps only the conversation-owned QUIT, Refresh,
and Send or ACK utility row; the internal keyboard also keeps its T9 and action
rows.  Inbound unread and hidden outbound-status badges remain independently
tappable when both exist.

Before staging, the conversation reserves one hidden protected record.  The
context is exactly nil, a string, a symbol, or an integer.  Sending makes the
reserved record visible before transport starts, and discard removes it.  A
ModemManager owner change makes both that record and the phone-global send
state unknown, so a delayed completion cannot authorize another send.

Conversation selection counts the final inert display bytes, including role
prefixes, visible control escapes, status suffixes, and separators, against the
64 KiB render bound.  Refresh progress, partial results, and terminal failures
remain visible in an open conversation as well as the catalog.  A refresh may
attach an exact outgoing modem object to the sole matching local sending
record, or to the sole matching sent record when no sending match exists.
Failed complete-refresh rollback preserves record identity; successful partial
snapshots update only same-owner modem records that are not newer than the
refresh cutoff.
