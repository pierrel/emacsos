#+title: Streaming transcript and SMS refresh-cutoff amendment
#+date: 2026-09-16

* Correction

The display-spoof boundary applies before every server-supplied assistant text
fragment is rendered.  It therefore covers canonical Assist snapshots and
history, provisional Assist Web SSE deltas, and local =/chat= NDJSON tokens.
Newline and tab remain valid multiline layout, and ZWJ and VS16 remain the only
admitted zero-width emoji format points.  A rejected live fragment terminates
its observation without inserting the fragment.

An outgoing modem snapshot from a complete refresh may attach only to a sole
matching local record whose revision is at or before that refresh's cutoff.
Thus an old modem snapshot cannot attach to or terminalize a newly created
identical local send.  A live ModemManager Added snapshot has no cutoff and
retains the existing exact-match attachment behavior.

* Verification

ERT drives hostile and admitted text through both live transports.  The SMS
interleaving fixture creates an identical local send after the refresh cutoff,
proves that the old snapshot remains a separate modem record, and proves that
the send's later failed lifecycle result remains authoritative.
