#+title: Phone remote-input bounds amendment
#+date: 2026-09-15

* Historical supersessions

This amendment replaces only the memory-only older-history conclusion in
[[file:2026-09-03-assist-web-thread-flow.org][Assist Web thread flow]], section /Step 2 implementation/, and the status-reader
bounding statement in [[file:2026-09-12-wifi-network-access.org][Wi-Fi network access]], section /Goal and scope/.  Those
records remain the historical designs for their respective features.

* Correction

The Assist Web client bounds both one canonical transcript and its cumulative
in-memory pagination state.  One message is at most 256 KiB.  One snapshot or
explicitly loaded history state is at most 500 messages and 1 MiB of message
text.  A recent-page refresh may preserve that already loaded older state, so
the final rendered union is independently capped at 1,000 messages and 2 MiB.
Any response or merge outside those limits is rejected before rendering and
the last valid buffer remains available.

The asynchronous network reader accepts at most 256 KiB and 2,048 output
lines.  A process filter accounts for the complete reader, not only the Wi-Fi
scan section.  Crossing either limit marks the attempt failed and kills the
reader process group; its sentinel never parses or publishes the partial
snapshot.  The earlier eight-second whole-process deadline remains in force.

* Verification

ERT covers oversized individual Assist messages, cumulative snapshot and
history limits, retained-history render limits, exact reader-filter bounds,
process-group termination, and fail-closed handling of an overflowed partial
snapshot.  The ordinary Assist history and network-reader suites remain green.
